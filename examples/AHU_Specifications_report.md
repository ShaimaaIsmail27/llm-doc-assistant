# Report: AHU_Specifications.pdf

## Summary

*   **Function and Scope:** Air Handling Units (AHUs) are factory-fabricated assemblies designed for circulating, cleaning, heating, cooling, humidifying, dehumidifying, or mixing air, requiring coordination with ductwork, controls, and piping systems.
*   **Construction Standards & Materials:** AHUs must be fabricated from solid, double-wall galvanized steel (minimum G90) and feature integral insulation; they are prohibited from being blow-through types that place coils downstream of the supply fans.
*   **Performance Requirements:** Units must adhere to strict performance criteria, including maximum capacity limits (23,600 L/s), specific fan operating point selection based on fan type, and mandatory vibration control measures for all fans.
*   **System Integration & Support:** Installation requires a self-supporting structure with heavy-duty steel bases, airtight sealing via specialized gaskets and blank-offs, and continuous double-wall stainless steel drain pans for condensate management.
*   **Compliance and Quality Assurance:** The system must comply with multiple industry standards (e.g., AHRI 430, AMCA 210, ASHRAE 51) and requires comprehensive submittals detailing full item descriptions, performance data, and custom drawings of the total assembled unit.
*   **Operational Controls & Components:** Critical components like dampers and mixing boxes must meet strict leakage rate requirements; fan arrays require Variable Speed Drives (VSDs), and multiple fans must modulate together in a pre-engineered structural frame.
*   **Testing and Commissioning:** Testing is mandatory, requiring written procedures submitted two weeks prior to factory testing. Final certification involves comprehensive system testing of performance, noise/vibration levels, and alignment across the entire operating range.
*   **Documentation Handover:** Final deliverables include complete O&M manuals (with wiring diagrams), a System Readiness Checklist, detailed training plans for operation and maintenance, and final sign-off contingent upon satisfactory resolution of all review comments.

**Safety notes:**
- The AHU assembly must include an electrical disconnect, lights, duplex receptacles, switches, wiring, utility connection points, unit support system, vibration isolators, and a drain pan.
- AHU casing shall be constructed such that there is no visible condensation on the exterior panels of the AHU located in the non-conditioned spaces.
- Welded exterior panels are prohibited.
- Latch shall be capable of restraining explosive opening of door with a force not less than 2000 Pa (8 inch WG).
- No cutting by torches will be allowed.
- The plenum fans shall be driven by variable speed drives with at least one back-up drive. Use of a drive with bypass is prohibited.
- Provide an actuator-controlled damper, manual blank off plate, and automatic back draft damper to isolate the fan not in operation due to failure.
- Redundancy requirement shall be met by all operating fans in an array and without the provision of an idle standby fan.
- A switch shall control the lights in each compartment with pilot light mounted outside the respective compartment access door.
- All wiring shall run in neatly installed electrical conduits and terminate in a junction box for field connection to the building system. Provide single point 115 volt - one phase connection at junction box.
- Provide factory or field mounted disconnect switch.
